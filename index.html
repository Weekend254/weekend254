FRESH AIR FIX
NATURE · KAREN

Oloolua Nature Trail

Explore indigenous forest trails, a waterfall, bamboo groves and the historic Mau Mau caves. Choose the 5 km or 10 km loop; allow 2–4 hours.

📅 Date: Saturday 14 & Sunday 15 November
🕒 Time: Daily, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM
📍 Location: Oloolua Forest, Karen, Nairobi
💰 Entry: Citizen adult KSh 200; child KSh 100. EAC resident adult KSh 400; non-resident adult KSh 600.
🎟️ Booking: Pay through KIPRE eCitizen; bring your receipt and original ID.
🌦️ Weather: Showers/thunderstorms possible later both days. Morning visit is the better bet; wear shoes with good grip and carry a light rain jacket.
