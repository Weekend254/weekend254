FOOD & DRINK · NAIROBI TRAIL

Nairobi Food Trail

Turn an afternoon into a flavour hunt. Follow a self-guided matcha trail across four Nairobi stops, with menu guidance and conversation games along the way.

📅 Date: Choose Saturday 21 or Sunday 22 November; select your start time when booking.
🕒 Time: About 2.5–3.5 hours.
📍 Location: Four stops across Lavington, Kilimani, Westlands and Sarit.
💰 Budget: Trail pass KSh 1,500 for up to 4 people (KSh 375 each when shared by 4), plus estimated food spend of KSh 2,000–3,500 per person. Approx. KSh 2,375–3,875 per person before transport.
🥤 Included: Digital route, four matcha stops, menu guidance, timing tips and conversation prompts. Food, drinks and transport are paid separately.
🎟️ Booking: Buy a trail pass and unlock the selected route; check the trail details and total spend before paying.

NATURE WALK · KAREN

Oloolua Nature Trail

Walk forest trails to the waterfall, bamboo grove and Mau Mau caves. Choose the 5 km or 10 km loop; allow 2–4 hours.

📅 Date: Saturday 21 & Sunday 22 November
🕒 Time: Daily, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM
📍 Location: Oloolua Forest, Karen, Nairobi
💰 Entry: Kenyan citizen adult KSh 200; child KSh 100. EAC resident adult KSh 400; child KSh 200. Non-resident adult KSh 600; child KSh 300.
🎟️ Booking: Book and pay through KIPRE eCitizen; carry your confirmation.
🌦️ Weather: Forest trails can be slippery after rain. Forecast suggests a possible morning shower; wear grippy shoes and recheck the forecast.
